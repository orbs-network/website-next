## Orbs and the network

### What is Orbs?

Orbs provides trading infrastructure for onchain finance, bringing aggregated liquidity, advanced spot orders and perpetual futures to venues, institutions and agents. Its Layer 3 network powers dSPOT, dPERPS and Execution Services alongside existing blockchains.

### What does Layer 3 mean here?

Orbs provides an additional execution layer alongside existing Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks. It runs application logic and services beyond what a smart contract can handle on its own, without requiring liquidity to move to a new chain.

### What are Orbs’ products?

Orbs has three products: [dSPOT](/dspot/) for spot execution, [dPERPS](/dperps/) for perpetual futures infrastructure, and [Execution Services](/execution-services/) for running application logic on the decentralised Orbs network.

## Trading and integration

### What does dSPOT include?

dSPOT combines aggregated liquidity with advanced spot order types, including limit, TWAP, stop loss and take profit. Features available to users depend on the venue and integration.

### How does TWAP work?

TWAP splits a larger order into smaller trades over time to help reduce price impact. Execution depends on market conditions and available liquidity.

### What is dPERPS?

dPERPS is Orbs’ perpetual futures infrastructure for venues and developers. It brings together the components needed to offer perpetual trading under a venue’s own brand.

### How can I integrate Orbs?

Choose a [white-label experience](/contact/), start building with the [integration documentation](https://docs.orbs.com/), or use [Orbs Agentic](/agentic/) for supported agent workflows. [Contact the team](/contact/) to select the right route for your use case.

### Does Orbs take custody of my assets?

dSPOT orders are signed from your wallet, and funds remain there until execution. dPERPS uses a self-custodial smart-contract trading model with collateral handled by the relevant contracts. The funding flow differs by product, so check the [documentation](https://docs.orbs.com/) for your integration.

### Which chains and venues are supported?

Orbs powers integrations across 30+ venues and 10+ chains. Product and feature availability vary by integration. See the [ecosystem page](/ecosystem/) and current [developer documentation](https://docs.orbs.com/) for details.

## Execution and staking

### What are Orbs Lambda and Orbs VM?

Lambda runs event-driven JavaScript functions. VM runs persistent services packaged as Docker containers. Both let developers extend onchain applications with decentralised execution on Orbs.

### What are Guardians and what is ORBS used for?

Guardians operate the network’s validator nodes. ORBS is the network’s native token and supports its proof-of-stake system. Token holders can delegate stake to Guardians; the [staking documentation](/pos/) explains participation and rewards.

### When did Orbs launch?

The project has been live since 2017, and mainnet has been live since 2019.
