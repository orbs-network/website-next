[PROCESS]

How it works

  1. 01

    The vote

    OIP-10 passed a Snapshot vote that closed Nov 18, 2026.

  2. 02

    The total

    507,540,603 ORBS will be burned in weekly batches.

  3. 03

    The source

    The ORBS come from the Orbs Long Term Reserves Pool and are held by the Orbs burn controller.

  4. 04

    The burn

    Each week the ORBS go to the dead address and the burn is recorded on Ethereum.

[METHODOLOGY]

How the weekly amount is calculated

  1. Price the week. Each Tuesday to Tuesday week (UTC) uses hourly ORBS/USD prices from CoinGecko, with no gap over 2 hours.
  2. Average it. The hourly prices give one time weighted average price for the week.
  3. Set the amount. ORBS burned = weekly USD reference ÷ average price. Lower price, more ORBS. Higher price, fewer ORBS.
  4. Burn it. The Orbs burn controller sends the ORBS to the dead address. Each burn shows in the history above.

Worked flow

Step 1 of 4

  1. 1Hourly ORBS/USD prices, Tuesday to Tuesday UTC, no gap over 2 hours
  2. 2Time weighted average price (P)
  3. 3ORBS amount = weekly USD reference ÷ P
  4. 4ORBS sent to the dead address, recorded on Ethereum

Methodology follows OIP-10.
